# Citations

Use these references when documenting algorithms, explaining numerical behavior, or comparing ChebyshevSharp with PyChebyshev.

## Chebyshev Interpolation

- Berrut, J.-P. and Trefethen, L. N. (2004). "Barycentric Lagrange Interpolation." *SIAM Review*, 46(3), 501-517. DOI: [10.1137/S0036144502417715](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0036144502417715).
- Trefethen, L. N. (2013). *Approximation Theory and Approximation Practice*. SIAM. Use for Chebyshev nodes, Bernstein ellipses, spectral convergence, and practical approximation guidance.
- Good, I. J. (1961). "The Colleague Matrix, a Chebyshev Analogue of the Companion Matrix." *The Quarterly Journal of Mathematics*, 12(1), 61-68. Use for Chebyshev root-finding background.

## Tensor Train Algorithms

- Oseledets, I. V. (2011). "Tensor-Train Decomposition." *SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing*, 33(5), 2295-2317. DOI: [10.1137/090752286](https://doi.org/10.1137/090752286).
- Oseledets, I. V. and Tyrtyshnikov, E. E. (2010). "TT-cross approximation for multidimensional arrays." *Linear Algebra and its Applications*, 432(1), 70-88. DOI: [10.1016/j.laa.2009.07.024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2009.07.024).
- Goreinov, S. A., Tyrtyshnikov, E. E., and Zamarashkin, N. L. (1997). "A theory of pseudoskeleton approximations." *Linear Algebra and its Applications*, 261(1-3), 1-21. Use for max-volume and pseudoskeleton approximation context.

## Finance Context

- Ruiz, I. and Zeron, M. (2022). *Machine Learning for Risk Calculations: A Practitioner's View*. Wiley Finance. Use for Chebyshev methods in risk, pricing, and Greeks workflows.

## Citation Style

- Prefer author-year citations in prose, for example "Berrut and Trefethen (2004)".
- Include DOI links for papers when available.
- Keep algorithm pages focused on what the reference supports: barycentric interpolation, spectral convergence, TT decomposition, TT-Cross, or max-volume pivoting.
