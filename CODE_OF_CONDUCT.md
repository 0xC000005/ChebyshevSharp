# Code of Conduct

ChebyshevSharp follows a simple contributor standard: keep discussions
respectful, technical, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and
  discussions.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when correctness or numerical behavior
  is unclear.
- Keep disagreement focused on code, tests, documentation, reproducibility, and
  project goals.
- Make room for new contributors by explaining context and linking relevant
  docs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic comments after a maintainer asks to refocus.
- Intentionally misleading reports, fabricated evidence, or bad-faith review
  behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments; close issues or pull requests;
or block participants who violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated
violations may be reported to GitHub.

This policy is intentionally short. When in doubt, follow the spirit of the
Contributor Covenant: participate in a way that keeps the community open,
welcoming, and useful.
